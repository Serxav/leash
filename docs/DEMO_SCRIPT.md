# Demo video script – draft

**Target: 2:45, never 3:00 or more.** Judges may stop at 3:00.

**Rules for the video**
- English narration, or English subtitles.
- The app runs on the real device: desktop and phone.
- No copyrighted music: none at all, or royalty-free music with a licence.
- No third-party brands or logos. Use generic products only (D-007, F3 decision).
- Upload to YouTube as **public**, not unlisted.

**Persona (invented):** Marta, office manager of a 6-person design studio in Barcelona,
with €150/month for office supplies.

| Time | Scene | What must be visible |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | **The problem:** nobody hands their wallet to an AI | Hook line + the Leash pitch: "Give your AI agent a budget, not your wallet" |
| 0:15–0:45 | **Rules:** Marta types her rules in plain language and confirms them as tags | The AI turns text into exact rules (Claude). Nothing applies until she confirms |
| 0:45–1:35 | **Agent restocks:** "We are out of printer paper and sticky notes, restock for the month" | Channel3 search. Two purchases are **APPROVED** and charged automatically through PayPal. One **NEEDS_APPROVAL**. The trap product with injected instructions is **BLOCKED**, with a one-sentence reason |
| 1:35–2:05 | **One-tap approval** on the phone | The charge appears in the PayPal sandbox Business account |
| 2:05–2:30 | **History** | The 14-day return window, a refund through PayPal, and revoking the mandate (vault token deleted) |
| 2:30–2:45 | **Closing** | Why it matters: humans stay in control, as EU payment rules require. "The AI proposes, the code decides" |
