# Demo video script

**Target: 2:45, never 3:00 or more.** Judges may stop at 3:00.

**Rules for the video**
- English narration, and English subtitles.
- The app runs on the real device: desktop browser and a phone.
- No copyrighted music: none at all, or royalty-free music with a licence.
- No third-party brands or logos: only the demo shops (`*-demo.example`, invented). If a
  Channel3 offer with a real shop name appears, cut it, unless Channel3 confirms on Discord
  that it is fine.
- Upload to YouTube as **public**, not unlisted.

**Before recording**
- Record on the live app (https://leash-crcx.onrender.com): open it a minute before, so it is
  awake. Press **Reset demo** for a clean space, then connect PayPal with the judges' sandbox
  Personal account.
- Desktop at 1280 × 800, light mode; phone in light mode.
- Have the PayPal sandbox Business account (the shop) open in another tab, to show the charge
  arrive.

**Persona (invented):** Marta, office manager of a 6-person design studio in Barcelona, with
€150 a month for office supplies.

| Time | Scene | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | **The problem** | Home page, Rex | "AI agents can already shop for us. But would you hand one your wallet? Leash gives your AI agent a budget, not your wallet." |
| 0:15–0:40 | **Rules** | **Rules**: Marta's rules as a list; then *Change your rules*, a tip, **Check my rules**, the draft list, **Confirm** | "Marta writes her rules in plain words. Meta's Muse Spark turns them into exact rules, and nothing applies until she confirms them. If something is missing, Leash asks instead of guessing." |
| 0:40–1:30 | **Rex restocks** | **Rex**: tap the paper and sticky notes suggestion; the working steps; the two ✓ cards; the summary; **Rules & activity → Security log** | "She asks Rex, the AI shopping agent. Rex searches Channel3 offers, but only inside her rules. Rex can only propose. Leash's own code, with no AI, decides each purchase. Paper and sticky notes are within budget, so PayPal charges them at once, with the PayPal account Marta saved once in the Vault. A cheaper offer from a shop she banned is skipped. And this product hides instructions for AI agents: plain code catches it before Rex ever sees it, and logs it." |
| 1:30–2:00 | **One-tap approval on the phone** | Phone: the €24 toner card / Inbox, **Approve**; then the PayPal sandbox Business account with the payment | "The toner costs more than Marta's ask-me-first amount, so it waits for her. One tap on her phone. Leash checks the price and her rules again, and only then PayPal Orders v2 charges it." |
| 2:00–2:30 | **History and control** | **History**: transaction ID, 14-day return window, **Refund**; **Permissions**: Pause Rex, Disconnect | "Every decision has its reason and its PayPal transaction. Refunds go through PayPal's Agent Toolkit, but only when Marta asks; Rex has no refund tool. She can pause Rex or disconnect PayPal at any moment, and PayPal deletes the saved token." |
| 2:30–2:45 | **Closing** | Home page, the three steps | "Humans stay in control, as EU payment rules require. The AI proposes, the code decides. That's Leash, built on PayPal, Meta, Channel3 and Render." |

**Must be named explicitly in the narration or on screen** (stage one is pass/fail):
- PayPal: Vault, Orders v2, refunds, Agent Toolkit
- Meta Muse Spark (the AI)
- Channel3 (and show it being called: the "Searching Channel3 offers" step)
- Render
